About Me

Mechanical engineer with a Master of Science degree in E-Mobilität (ACES) from FAU Erlangen-Nürnberg, specialised in AI & Autonomous Driving and E-Powertrain, and a peer-reviewed IEEE publication. Combines a solid engineering foundation with hands-on AI development, from training models to deploying them on edge hardware.

My engineering background shapes how I work. Managing overhaul operations with 50+ technicians, I was already doing the core loop: collecting data, spotting anomalies, making decisions and reporting findings. My move into AI is about automating and scaling that same loop.

I think in systems, not just models. Real hardware and real constraints taught me that deployment and reliability matter as much as accuracy. Reliable, improvement-minded and comfortable taking responsibility, I'm looking for a role where I can turn academic knowledge into working products and keep growing.
